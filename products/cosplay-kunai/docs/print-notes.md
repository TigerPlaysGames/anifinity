# Cosplay Kunai — P1S print notes

Version 3 is a **blunt, nonfunctional costume prop**, approximately 280 mm long. Its proportions were measured across several straight-on references for the licensed 28–29 cm Naruto Shippuden replicas: approximately 52% faceted blade, 32% wrapped grip, and 16% ring assembly. The full-length model fits a Bambu Lab P1S build plate when rotated diagonally.

Suggested starting profile:

- Material: PLA or PLA Basic
- Orientation: rotate roughly 45 degrees on the build plate; place one broad blade facet down
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Walls: 4
- Infill: 15% gyroid
- Top/bottom layers: 5
- Supports: build-plate-only support may be needed beneath the handle and ring because the blade has a full convex cross-section
- Brim: optional, 3–5 mm if bed adhesion is inconsistent

The model uses overlapping watertight shells for the blade, guard, grip bands, connector, and ring. Bambu Studio should merge them when slicing; verify that the preview shows continuous toolpaths before printing.

Keep the prop blunt. Do not sharpen, reinforce with metal, throw it, or carry it where weapon-like props are prohibited. Check convention and venue rules before bringing it in public.

For commercial listings, use an original anime-inspired version and original branding rather than franchise names, logos, or copied reference art.
