# Prototype checklist

- [ ] Print a short peg-and-socket tolerance sample first
- [ ] Confirm the white shell seats without cracking or looseness
- [ ] Test a small phone and a large phone in portrait orientation
- [ ] Test both phones with their normal protective cases
- [ ] Confirm the phone does not tip during screen taps
- [ ] Confirm the retaining lips do not cover the screen
- [ ] Test charging-cable insertion and removal
- [ ] Check the cable is not sharply bent
- [ ] Inspect all contact edges for sharp corners or roughness
- [ ] Photograph the real prototype for the primary marketplace image
- [ ] Record actual filament weight and print time
- [ ] Update listing dimensions and compatibility from the physical sample
